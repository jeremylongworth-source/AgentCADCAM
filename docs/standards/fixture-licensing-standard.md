# Fixture and Third-Party Licensing Standard

## Synthetic fixtures

Synthetic geometry, metadata, and expected outputs created for this repository should be marked with a clear license. The initial CAD bracket fixture is released as `CC0-1.0 synthetic fixture` unless a fixture manifest states otherwise.

## Third-party material

Do not add CAD, NC/G-code, machine profiles, manuals, screenshots, models, code, or datasets unless redistribution and modification rights are documented. Preserve the original license, attribution, source locator, version, and any notice requirements in the fixture manifest.

## Restricted material

Confidential, proprietary, export-sensitive, credential-bearing, or machine-network data must not be committed. When a real input is needed for evaluation, keep it in a controlled external evaluation packet and commit only a sanitized manifest and expected outcome.

## Review

Fixture licensing is a repository-readiness check, not legal advice. Ambiguous rights produce `SOURCE_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` and block publication of the material.

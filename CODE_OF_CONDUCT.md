# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and safety-conscious community. Participants should communicate constructively, distinguish evidence from assumptions, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or reckless manufacturing guidance.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence.
- Protect confidential, proprietary, and safety-sensitive information.
- Flag unsafe or misleading behavior promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, credential sharing, malicious code, deliberate data exfiltration, and instructions to bypass machine safeguards are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where practical. Maintainers may remove content, request correction, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action.
